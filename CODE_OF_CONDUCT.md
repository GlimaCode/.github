# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a small studio. We keep it simple: treat everyone here — maintainers,
contributors, and anyone opening an issue — with respect.

**Expected:** clear and courteous communication, good-faith questions,
constructive criticism of code rather than of people, and accepting that a
maintainer may decline a change without it being personal.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies to all spaces in this organization — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and commit messages — and to anyone representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **glimacode.studio@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately. Depending on severity, responses range from a warning to a permanent
block from the organization.

Maintainers are held to the same standard as everyone else.
